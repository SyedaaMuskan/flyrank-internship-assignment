# What I Learned

The main lesson from this assignment is that a portfolio is not just a collection of pages.

Every page should answer three questions:

1. What should the visitor understand?
2. What proof should they see?
3. Where should they go next?

The strongest work should appear first, every case should support the same overall story, and missing proof should be identified before the portfolio is built.

> **The goal is not to show everything I have done. The goal is to make the right work easy to understand and easy to trust.**
