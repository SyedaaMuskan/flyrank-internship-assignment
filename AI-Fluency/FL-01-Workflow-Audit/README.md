# 📖 Overview

This assignment focuses on understanding how I currently work and how Artificial Intelligence can become an effective engineering partner rather than simply an answer generator.

The objective is to analyze my real weekly workflow, identify where AI creates the most value, define where human judgment remains essential, and establish a structured collaboration process that will be refined throughout the FlyRank Internship.

---

# 🎯 Assignment Objectives

- Audit my real weekly workflow.
- Classify recurring tasks based on the appropriate level of AI involvement.
- Configure a Claude Project tailored to my internship and learning goals.
- Complete the Anthropic Academy **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** course.
- Select three recurring tasks to improve throughout future AI Fluency assignments.

# 💭 Reflection

This assignment helped me think more intentionally about how I collaborate with AI. Instead of viewing AI as a tool for generating answers, I now see it as an engineering partner that can assist with research, implementation, debugging, and documentation while keeping critical thinking and final decision-making in my hands.

The workflow audit also highlighted the importance of distinguishing between tasks that should remain human-driven and those where AI can safely accelerate productivity. This mindset will guide how I use AI throughout my internship and future software engineering projects.

# 🚀 Key Takeaways

- AI is most valuable when used as a collaborative engineering partner.

- Documentation and reflection are as important as implementation.
- Effective AI collaboration requires verification, critical thinking, and continuous improvement.
